# {{TOPIC}} heuristics

This file gives quick heuristics for forming candidate answers before formal
checking.

Heuristics are not final answers. Every candidate must be checked against the
task specification and answer rubric.

## Diagnostic questions

1. What is the target?
2. What assumptions control the answer?
3. What changes across regimes?
4. What known problem is closest?
5. What danger zones apply?

## When heuristics fail

Heuristics fail when assumptions, targets, losses, support conditions, or
regularity conditions differ from the familiar case.
